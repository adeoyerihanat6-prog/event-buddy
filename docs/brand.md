# Event Buddy - Brand Guidelines & Design System

## Color Palette
* **Primary Background:** `#0B0B0F` (Deep dark slate)
* **Card / Surface Background:** `#17171C` (Muted dark gray)
* **Primary Accent (CTA/Highlights):** `#FF6B6B` (Vibrant coral pink/red)
* **Secondary Accent (Badges/Stars):** `#FFD166` (Warm golden yellow)
* **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white)
* **Text Secondary:** `#9CA3AF` (Gray 400 for descriptions)

## Typography
* **Font Family:** Inter or system-ui (Clean sans-serif)
* **Headings:** Bold to Black weight (700 - 900)
* **Body:** Regular to Medium weight (400 - 500)

## Tone of Voice
* Fun, high-energy, casual, and community-driven. 
* Emphasizes safety, inclusivity, and making genuine local connections.
